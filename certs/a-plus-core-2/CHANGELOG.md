# Provo A+ Core 2 changelog

## v1.0.1
- Mini-exams now include performance-based questions, as on the real exam. The 10-, 20-, and 40-question exams start with 1, 2, or 4 PBQs, spread across the domains, and a new All PBQs option runs every PBQ back to back.
- Each PBQ is worth 2 multiple-choice questions, scored by the share of items you get right. Time is 1 minute per multiple-choice question plus 6 minutes per PBQ.
- You can flag a PBQ and come back to it after the multiple choice; your answers are kept. If time runs out, PBQs are graded as they stand.
- The results page scores each PBQ and reviews every PBQ item with its answer and explanation, before the multiple-choice review.
- PBQs finished in an exam count toward each PBQ's clears. The All PBQs exam counts toward the Mini-Exams rank, like the 20- and 40-question exams.

## v1.0.0
- First full release of Provo A+ Core 2 for CompTIA A+ Core 2 220-1202. All 14 modules are open and cover every objective.
- **Acronyms:** 101 exam acronyms, asked three ways, with a Type it mode.
- **Objective modules (02 to 10):** 295 terms in 38 topics, from the command line and Windows tools to security, hardening, and operational procedures. Each term is asked several ways, with wrong answers chosen to be the same kind of thing as the right one. The two troubleshooting modules ask for the usual fix, from the problem's name and from a scenario.
- **Question Qualifiers:** 50 help desk questions, 10 each for FIRST, NEXT, BEST, MOST likely, and MINIMUM.
- **Exam-Style Drill:** 208 original exam-style questions across all four domains and 36 skills, in four styles: best fit, scenario, exhibit, and direct. One question in four in each objective module comes from this bank.
- **Performance-Based Questions:** seven simulations, a new variation every run: malware removal in order, the command line, Windows tools, a SOHO router audit, share and NTFS permissions, backups and restores, and a help desk queue.
- **Mini-Exams:** 10, 20, or 40 questions, weighted 28 / 28 / 23 / 21 to the official domains.
- **Cram Sheet:** the malware removal steps, commands, Windows tools, editions and file systems, macOS and Linux, security, troubleshooting, and operational procedures on one page.
