# Provo Linux Essentials changelog

## v1.0.0
- First release: a study lab for LPI Linux Essentials (exam 010, objectives version 2.0), in 16 modules across seven stages.
- 48 acronyms and 316 terms in 11 modules, one or two per objective group, with a type-the-answer mode for commands.
- 205 exam-style questions across all 19 objectives, in four styles (direct, scenario, exhibit, best fit). Every command and output was checked on a live Linux system.
- The two other LPI formats: 30 multiple-response (choose two) and 60 short-input (type it exactly) questions, with their own practice module.
- Mini-exams of 10, 20, or 40 questions built to LPI's blueprint, one question per objective weight point, mixing all three formats. Your first exam is saved as your baseline.
- A Readiness Exam of 40 held-out questions that appear nowhere else in the app, with a study record that compares your baseline, recent mini-exams, and first Readiness Exam score.
